Working Paper

So close and yet so far: The ability of mandatory disclosure rules to crack down on offshore tax evasion

Elisa Casi, Mohammed Mardan & Rohit Reddy Muddasani

Examines the effects of DAC6 on offshore tax evasion using bilateral cross-border deposit data and documents responses to differences in enforcement and reporting obligations.
